Members (and customers) can now give their own AI agent a key that acts as them. Under "Your agent" in member settings, they choose what it may do (read their feed, conversations and notifications; post and react; send messages) and for how long (7, 30 or 90 days). The key is shown once and can be stopped at any time. A personal key never goes beyond what its person could do. It can never use the site's staff tools or take actions that need a human. It only sees the tools it was given over MCP, and owners can still see and revoke every key.
