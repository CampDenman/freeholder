You can now hire things out. Mark a catalogue product as a rental, give it its
own resource calendar, and Freeholder handles the rest: by the hour, day or
week, with a deposit, a turnaround gap for cleaning or charging, and a
replacement value for the day something does not come back.

Because a hireable thing has a real calendar, its time shows up in your diary
beside everything else you have booked — and the same protection that stops two
people booking one room at once stops two people hiring one lens. There is no
separate availability to keep in step.

The hire desk at **Hire** shows what is out, what is late, and what is due
back, overdue first. Hand something over, take it back, and Freeholder works
out the late fee by the unit and what the damage policy says — then tells you
what goes back of the deposit and what is still owed, without moving a penny by
itself. Settling stays yours to do on the invoice.

Prices come from your normal price lists, so a member rate or a volume break
applies to hire exactly as it applies to a sale.
