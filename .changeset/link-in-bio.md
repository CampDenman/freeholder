You can now have a link-in-bio page on your own site instead of renting one. Admin → Link in bio creates it (at /links unless you choose another address): your picture or logo, your name, a short bio, the links you choose in the order you choose, and your social icons. A link can go anywhere on the web, or straight to one of your own products, bookable services, events, galleries, pages or the newsletter sign-up — and those follow your site, so a link to something you unpublish, a gallery that expires or a service that is no longer bookable quietly drops off instead of leading to a dead end. Each link can be hidden without deleting it, or scheduled to appear and disappear at times you set in your own time zone. The page uses your site's colours and fonts (with solid, outline and soft button styles that keep readable contrast in light and dark), works with a keyboard and a screen reader, and has its own title, description, social card and canonical address like any other page. You can also edit it in the visual page editor, where the links are a list you can reorder, hide and schedule. Every click goes through your own site and is counted by your own analytics, under the same consent rules as everything else — no outside tracker — and the screen shows clicks and visitors per link; for links to your own pages, the visits and enquiries they lead to show up in your traffic reports too. Your AI assistant can do all of this through the API and MCP tools (`linkinbio.*`), with the same permissions and audit trail as you. The sample site now includes one at /links.
