Your site is now an MCP server, so an AI assistant can work in your business
directly. Point one at `/api/mcp` with an API key and it discovers what your
business can do — your modules, your plugins, this version — and can then do
those things: add a contact, publish a page, look up your opening hours.

What it is offered depends on the key you gave it. A key that can only read
contacts sees only the tools that read contacts, so an assistant is never
guessing at what it is allowed to do or being refused halfway through a task.
Everything it does is recorded against that key by name in your activity log,
alongside everything you and your staff did.

Tools are marked read-only or destructive, so an assistant that asks before
doing something irreversible knows which those are.

Signing in and managing keys are deliberately not offered as tools. An
assistant uses the key you gave it; issuing credentials stays your job.
