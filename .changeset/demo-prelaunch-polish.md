The dashboard's "What changed" now reads as plain business news in every language: invoices issued, viewed and paid, messages received and replied to, appointments made, moved and cancelled, orders, payments, refunds and payouts each get their own line, and so do credit notes, price and tax changes, subscriptions, invitations, webhooks, connected accounts, sign-ins and password changes, API keys, team roles and access grants. Anything else says which part of your site changed and who changed it (you or a teammate, an API key or agent, a visitor, or Freeholder itself) instead of showing internal wording. The Shop page (/products) now shows each product's picture and its "From" price with an add-to-cart button, the same card collection pages use, and collection pages gained the picture too. Invoice lines now show their amount before tax and before any discount, so the lines add up to the subtotal printed beneath them, with the discount and tax on their own rows. In the admin, an invoice line reads "quantity × price = amount" exactly as entered, with its before-tax amount and any line discount on separate labelled lines when they differ. A service with no cancellation policy now says so the same way on the booking page and the appointment page. The inbox shows the teammate a conversation is assigned to by name rather than by email address. In the public demo, the order confirmation no longer offers a portal sign-in the demo cannot send, store pickup names the Courtenay studio, and products and events offer a small share option (copy link, share sheet, email) instead of ten buttons.
