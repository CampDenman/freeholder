What someone may use is now a grant, not a flag on the page. A membership,
a class pass, a one-time unlock, a loyalty tier or a decision you make by
hand all grant the same kind of access, and the site asks that question at
the moment of looking rather than trusting a leftover checkbox.

A membership starts a site-wide grant for the period already paid for.
Pausing it takes the access away; cancelling at period end leaves it until
the period runs out. Member-only products hide from strangers and show to
whoever holds a grant. You can also grant and revoke access by hand from
Access in admin.
