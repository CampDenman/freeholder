Quotes are here. Draft an offer with as many lines as it needs, mark some of
them optional so the customer can choose, and send it — they get a private link
with no account to create.

They can read it, tick the extras they want and watch the total update, ask a
question, accept it by typing their name, or say no with a reason you can
actually learn from. You see when they opened it, which is usually the moment
worth following up on.

Every version is kept. If you revise a quote — because they asked, or because
you had a better idea — the old lines stay exactly where they were, so "you
quoted me a different price" has an answer rather than an argument. A quote
already with the customer cannot be quietly edited: the only way to change it
is a revision they can see, and the link they already have opens it.

What they accept is frozen at the moment they accept it, down to which optional
lines they chose. Quotes with a date on them lapse on their own and land on a
list to follow up.
