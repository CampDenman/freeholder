`freeholder launch` stands up a new hosted Freeholder site on DigitalOcean with one command: storage buckets with a key scoped to just them, a droplet built from the recipe, private generated secrets, the running site, confirmed backups, and finally a one-click link for the owner to claim it. It can be re-run to continue after an interruption, cut over from a temporary address to your domain once DNS is set, and tear down what it created.
