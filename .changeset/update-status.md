Your instance now remembers what the update feed offered, so it can tell you
whether you are exposed even when the feed is unreachable. One status line
answers it — "Up to date", "Update available", or "2 security releases behind
— CVSS 8.1" — and it says "no check has completed yet" rather than "up to
date" when it genuinely does not know. It also tells you the earliest version
you could still roll back to.
