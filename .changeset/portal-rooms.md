Your customers can now sign in and see the whole relationship in one place:
their quotes, agreements, invoices, bookings, orders, hires, projects and
messages, each in its own room.

What they see is read through the very same queries you use in admin, filtered
to them — so a figure in the portal and the same figure on your screen cannot
drift apart, because there is only one of them.

A room only appears when there is something in it, and if a room cannot be
loaded it says so rather than quietly looking empty. Rooms for things that
arrive later — subscriptions, passes, referral earnings, shared files — will
appear on their own as those features are built.
