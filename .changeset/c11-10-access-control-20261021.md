Tightened who can do what in several places customers and visitors reach:

- Phone and upload-link uploads now work only while their capture link is open.
  A closed, expired or made-up link no longer adds files to the media library.
- Customers changing their own membership plan see and can choose only the
  public plans sold by the same seller. A switch that would cost more right away
  now takes effect at the next renewal, when it is billed like any other period,
  so nobody gets the dearer plan before paying for it. Plan changes onto a plan
  with no price in the subscription's currency are refused instead of billing
  nothing.
- Joining a card-required trial that has a setup fee always raises the setup-fee
  invoice first, and only a card the customer saved themselves can start
  membership billing.
- On quotes, only your staff can reply as the business. A signed-in customer
  always speaks as the customer and needs the quote link.
- Starting a website chat with somebody else's email address opens a separate
  conversation, so it cannot take over an existing chat or see your replies to
  the real person.
- Undoing a contact import no longer overwrites fields your team edited after
  the import ran.
- Agreement signatures record the address and browser the request actually came
  from, not values supplied by the caller.
- Call relay credentials go only to members with a call ringing or in progress.
- Repeatedly claiming encryption key packages hands back the same package
  instead of using up another member's supply.
- Briefings and colleagues' shared list views are visible only to staff.
- Trying coupon codes at checkout is limited per shopper.
