Tightened who can do what where customers and visitors reach the site. Phone and upload-link uploads work only while their capture link is open, so a closed or made-up link no longer adds files to the media library. Customers changing their own membership see and can choose only the public plans sold by the same seller; a switch that costs more right away now starts at the next renewal and is billed then, and a plan with no price in the subscription's currency is refused instead of billed at zero. A card-required trial with a setup fee always raises the setup-fee invoice first, and only a card the customer saved themselves can start membership billing. Only your staff reply as the business on quotes. Starting a website chat with somebody else's email address opens a separate conversation, so it cannot take over an existing chat or see your replies. Undoing a contact import keeps fields your team edited since. Agreement signatures record the address and browser the request really came from. Call relay credentials go only to members with a call ringing or in progress, and repeated encryption key claims no longer use up another member's supply. Briefings and colleagues' shared list views are visible only to staff, and trying coupon codes at checkout is limited per shopper.
