Tightened who can do what where customers and visitors reach the site. Phone and upload-link uploads work only while their capture link is open, so a closed or made-up link no longer adds files to the media library. Customers changing their own membership see and can choose only the public plans sold by the same seller. When the plan charges the difference straight away, the new plan starts as soon as that invoice is paid; a change that waits for the end of the period now bills the new plan's price from the next renewal; and a plan with no price in the subscription's currency is refused instead of billed at zero. A card-required trial with a setup fee always raises the setup-fee invoice first, and only a card the customer saved themselves can start membership billing. Only your staff reply as the business on quotes. A website chat started by someone who has not signed in opens its own conversation, marked "Unverified — says they are …" in your inbox, so typing a customer's email address can never take over their chat or receive your replies to them. Signed-in customers still continue their own conversation. Undoing a contact import keeps fields your team edited since. Agreement signatures record the address and browser the request really came from. Call relay credentials go only to members with a call ringing or in progress, and repeated encryption key claims no longer use up another member's supply. Briefings and colleagues' shared list views are visible only to staff, and trying coupon codes or opening carts is limited per shopper.
