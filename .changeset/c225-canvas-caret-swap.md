Typing on the canvas right after a save no longer loses letters. When an edit saved, the canvas loaded the new version out of sight and swapped it in the moment it was ready — even if you had already clicked into the next paragraph and were typing, so the rest of what you typed went into a copy of the page you could no longer see. The canvas now waits for you to finish (press Enter, click elsewhere, or leave the canvas) before it swaps, and a click on the canvas during that moment — on a replace-image button, say — still does what you pressed.
