One inbox for everything anybody has said to you. Emails, texts, contact form
submissions and chat all land in the same list, threaded by person rather than
scattered across four places to check.

Four things you can do to a conversation, and that is deliberately all: say
whose it is, put it away until a date, close it, or reply. No folders to
maintain, no labels to invent — this is a list of what is waiting, not a second
email client to learn.

Put something away for Tuesday and it comes back on Tuesday, marked unread, as
though nobody had touched it. That is the whole promise of snoozing, and the
version that quietly never returns is just closing with extra steps.

Replies go out the way the person got in touch, so whoever texted you gets a
text back and nobody has to pick. If a channel is not connected yet, it says so
rather than filing a reply that never left.

Search finds a conversation by a fragment of what was actually said — half a
word is enough — or by who said it. Tick several and close, hand over or snooze
them together.
