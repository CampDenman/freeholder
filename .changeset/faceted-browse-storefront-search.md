Faceted storefront browse and product search — the shopper-facing half of the C3.24 parity mapping's G1 gap (C3.25 slice 2). One public service, `catalog.browseProducts`, powers both surfaces: filter/sort/pagination over live rows (active + public, the global search registry's participation rules) for a collection or a search term. Facet dimensions derive from the catalog's existing primitives with no new tables: option types/values, filterable attribute definitions (value counts; observed ranges for number/measure), an anonymous-mirror price (sale beats retail; audience-gated lists don't apply; half-open ladder bands), and per-product availability mirroring `catalog.availability`. Counts are conjunctive — a selected dimension reads the scope without its own filter. The collection page at `/c/<slug>` gains the filter panel and sort links; `/search` becomes the public product search. Filter state lives in query params per the SEO doctrine: facet links are crawlable anchors and GET forms (no client script), filtered views are noindexed with canonical on the clean address, and robots.txt covers the bracket grammar.
