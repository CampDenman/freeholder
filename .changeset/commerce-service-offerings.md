Service products now carry real offering configuration: duration, buffers,
location type, capacity, assignment, travel time, a deposit (fixed or
percent), a reusable cancellation policy and an optional intake form. You can
also say how the service may be paid — in full, deposit then balance, a
payment plan, hourly or a retainer — and the catalog can quote the deposit
and balance from the same price resolver. Calendars and signed waivers still
attach when scheduling and contracts exist.
