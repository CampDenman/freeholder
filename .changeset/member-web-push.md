Members can now get push notifications in their browser, even when the site isn't open. They turn them on under Notifications, and can switch push off per topic. Push is standard Web Push under the site's own keys, with no third-party push vendor, and messages are encrypted so the browser's push service can't read them. Members can also set quiet hours: during that window, push and email (digests included) wait until it ends. Critical alerts and the in-app inbox are never held.
