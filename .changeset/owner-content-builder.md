Freeholder now includes the first complete owner-facing self-builder lane.
Describe a site-wide content outcome in one brief and the configured builder
returns a typed, rendered before/after proposal across pages and shared chrome.
Nothing changes until the owner approves it. Approval applies the exact
previewed operations atomically through the ordinary CMS services, records the
builder as the author, and offers one-click rollback; proposals fail stale
instead of overwriting newer human work.

The builder is available in admin, through the generated HTTP API and MCP
tools, and from a signed-in-owner affordance on the public site. Site content
is isolated as untrusted reference data, `builder.*` remains separate from
workforce scopes, model usage has visible hard budgets, and the PM Brain
adapter uses a purpose-bound Paradise Modern site credential.

The CMS also gains an editable phone-callout block with separate E.164 routing
and human-readable display fields for prominent, accessible click-to-call
lines in pages or shared chrome.
