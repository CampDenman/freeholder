There is an admin now. Sign in at /login and you land on an overview showing
how many contacts you have and what stage they are at, plus a plain-English
list of everything that has changed on your site — including anything an AI
agent did on your behalf, named by the key it used.

Settings are editable after setup, not just during it, so your business
details, currency, time zone, languages and units are no longer fixed by the
choices you made in the first two minutes. Every time zone and currency is
selectable, not a short list.

Signing out really ends the session on the server rather than just forgetting
it in your browser, and the admin tells search engines not to index it.
