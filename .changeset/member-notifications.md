Members are now notified when someone follows them or asks to, sends or accepts a friend request, mentions or tags them, comments on their post or replies to their comment, or reacts to their post. Notifications appear on the new Notifications page in the portal, and each member chooses per kind of activity whether to see it on the site and whether to get it by email immediately, in a daily or weekly digest, or not at all. Member email defaults to a digest, reactions on one post fold into a single entry, and nothing ever arrives from someone you blocked or muted.
