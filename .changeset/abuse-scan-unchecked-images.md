"freeholder": patch
---

An image held because the abuse-imagery check could not run, for example
because of a timeout or because it was too large, is no longer treated as
evidence. The owner can rescan or delete it like any other held file. Only an
actual match is placed under legal hold, including files held earlier under
the old tagging. Every upload of a matched image is linked to its own uploader.
Matches are recorded for the legal report even when the members module is
off, and an upload is refused if its match could not be recorded.
