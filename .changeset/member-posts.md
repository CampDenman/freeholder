Members can now post. Each post has its own audience (everyone, followers and friends, friends only, paying subscribers or only the author) and supports threaded comments, six reactions, @mentions, #hashtags, sharing public posts, and tagging people, who approve the tag before it appears. The new Feed shows posts from people you follow and your friends, newest first, with nothing ranked or hidden by an algorithm; a "latest public posts" view and hashtag pages are also strictly chronological. Mutes keep someone out of your feed, blocks hide posts and comments both ways, and a restricted member's comments are visible only to them and the post's author. Every action is available to agents through the API and MCP.
