Your customers' phones can now be registered for push. A device is registered
against the contact like any other way of reaching them, so the same per-topic
preferences apply and nothing gets pushed that would not also have been
emailed. Booking confirmed, gallery ready, invoice due and back-in-stock are
now topics people can turn on or off individually. A phone handed on to
somebody else moves rather than quietly notifying the wrong person, signing out
stops the pushes, and merging two duplicate contacts keeps both their devices.
