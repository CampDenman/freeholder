You can now decide who is allowed to book you, and on what terms.

The example this is built around: customers can book you during shop hours,
your friends can book you any time, and your dentist appointment blocks both
without telling anybody it is a dentist appointment.

An audience says how someone proves they belong — anyone at all, a private
link, a tag on their contact record, or simply being signed in — and then which
hours apply to them, which services they can book, which calendar the booking
lands on, and how much notice they need to give. Set it once per group.

Busy time always blocks everybody. That is not a setting and never will be: a
booking system that can double-book you is worse than none.

A private link is a credential, so it is handed over once rather than sitting
on the page, and you can rotate it — the old link stops working immediately.

An audience you have not given any services to cannot book anything, on
purpose. And removing an audience never touches appointments already made:
those are in someone's diary.
