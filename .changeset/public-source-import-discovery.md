Read a public WordPress API, sitemap, feed or HTML site directly in the import
studio. Discovery respects robots rules and public network, page, byte and
rate limits, verifies complete WordPress pagination, and stages drafts for
review before publication. A delayed discovery cannot overwrite a changed
import, and a committed import cannot be replaced with a new preview.

Exact operation-scoped keys retain their identity through explicitly bound
private orchestration phases. Private phases remain absent from HTTP/MCP/SDK;
they cannot borrow authority from another actor, operation or completed run.

This does not complete generic/archive migration or source-specific cutover
acceptance (MASTER C3.22/C3.23/C11.10).
