Membership trials select cards from their original payment account and retain their provider when defaults change. Signed Stripe Connect feedback now reaches the private payment ledger, and recurring seller payments require the actual charge, fee and period evidence. Direct seller earnings are recorded as already paid, with refunds and disputes reconciled against the original charge, so they cannot trigger a second payout. Seller-as-merchant activation remains unavailable while its remaining integration and provider acceptance are completed (C12.38).
