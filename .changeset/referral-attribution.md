Know where your customers actually came from. Give a referrer a code, and every
way somebody can arrive with it — a link, a QR code scanned at a market stall,
a code typed at the till, an invitation followed — is recorded in one place, on
your own server, with no third party involved.

A click counts even when it happened long before the person told you who they
were: the visit is matched to them when they finally get in touch.

You choose how credit is awarded — the first touch, the last, or split across
the whole journey — and you can change your mind. Because every touch is kept,
changing the rule re-reads your history rather than rewriting it, and last
month's answer updates with it.

Nobody can refer themselves, and there are no chains of referrers earning off
each other — the shape of the data refuses it rather than a policy asking
nicely.
