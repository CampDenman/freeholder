Give customers a portal to sign into, rather than a folder of emails to keep.
One home with your details, your language, your privacy choices and the devices
you are signed in on — reachable by a link sent to your email or by a password,
whichever you prefer.

You can correct your own name and phone number. Your email stays as the
business knows it, because that is what ties your history together.
