Ads now count first-party. An impression is recorded when the visible
creative renders; a viewable impression after half of it has been on
screen for one continuous second. Daily totals, unique visitors, clicks
and spend roll up for the advertiser report. Even pacing and frequency
caps come from those numbers. A per-thousand or per-click campaign can
be reconciled against what actually delivered, on the same invoice.
