Your calendar now goes both ways. Every booking calendar gets a subscribable
.ics link you can paste into Apple Calendar, Google, Outlook or anything else
that reads a feed — rotatable in one click if it ever leaks — and every
confirmed appointment carries an attachment the customer can add to their own
diary, including a cancellation that removes it again. You can also block your
availability from any .ics feed somebody else publishes, so a calendar you
don't own still keeps you from being booked over. None of this needs a
connected account.

For owners who have connected Google or Microsoft, appointments now appear on
the real calendar, move when they are rescheduled and disappear when they are
cancelled — only on calendars you marked bookable, and only with calendar
writing switched on. Freeholder writes the bookings it made and nothing else.
An appointment that syncs back as busy time is recognised as its own
reflection, so it never blocks its own hour twice.
