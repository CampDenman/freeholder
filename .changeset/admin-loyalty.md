Loyalty has its own admin screen. Set up the programme, say what earns points and how many, define tiers and rewards, see recent redemptions, and correct a balance with a reason that goes on the ledger row.

What points are costing you is the first thing on the page, not the last: outstanding points valued at your own redemption rate, across however many customers hold them.

Two gaps this turned up are fixed with it: there was no way to read back the earn rules you had configured, and editing a programme silently reset its minimum account age.
