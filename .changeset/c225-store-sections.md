Store sections turn a page into a storefront without leaving the editor. Three new blocks compose live catalog data onto any page: the collection showcase shelves a collection's current membership, the product row shows the products you pick in your order, and the promotional band pairs your copy and call to action with a collection's live shelf and a full-width band behind it. Every shelf draws through the same public components the storefront's own pages ship, so a product added to a collection appears on your landing page the moment the page is requested — no republish, no stale roster. On the canvas the sections behave like everything else you edit: headings and button labels type where they render, a button on the section opens a picker to swap its collection or re-pick its products, and the change saves and shows immediately. The canvas itself got the audit's remaining editor fixes: structural edits reload the frame invisibly instead of flashing it, undo and redo are visible buttons with a history menu that lists every edit, images can be given alt text where they render, and a focus mode gives editing the whole screen with the page at its true height, persistent block outlines and a zoom control.
