Sites can now pay sellers through Stripe Connect, PayPal Payouts or Razorpay Route instead of by hand. The owner chooses the provider under Admin → Members; sellers set up their payout account from Payouts (Stripe's own pages, their PayPal email, or their bank details for Razorpay), and each payout is sent automatically when it falls due, once, and reversed through the provider if the buyer is refunded in full. A payout the provider keeps refusing is handed to the owner to review.
