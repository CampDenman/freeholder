Documents can be sent to a client and their history stands up afterwards. A document is revised rather than replaced, so "which version did they sign" and "what did we send them in March" both have answers; a share either follows the current version or is pinned to the one it was sent about.

Sharing uses the same vocabulary as a gallery — a link, a password or a signed-in customer, with a stated expiry and a download policy — so an owner learns one model rather than two.

Every open is on the record, and so is every refusal: an expired link, a revoked one, a wrong password and a spent download limit each leave a row saying which it was. The visitor is told only that it did not open. The owner can export a document with its versions, its shares and its whole access history.

Customers see the documents shared with them in their portal.
