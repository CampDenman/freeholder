You can now see your own traffic: how many people visited, what they read,
where they came from, and which of them became enquiries. It is counted by your
own server rather than by a script in your visitors' browsers, so ad blockers
do not change the numbers and nothing about your audience is sent anywhere. And
when somebody fills in a form, the pages they read *before* deciding to get in
touch are attached to their contact record.
