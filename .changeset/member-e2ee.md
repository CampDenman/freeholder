Member messages are now end-to-end encrypted. Each browser where a member opens their messages becomes one of their devices, with its own keys, and once everyone in a conversation has a device only those devices can read it — the site stores ciphertext. Conversations show when they are encrypted, and say so plainly when someone has not set up a device yet. Members can see and remove their devices in their settings; a removed device is taken out of every conversation before anyone sends again. Reporting an encrypted message shares exactly that message with moderators, verified so it cannot be altered. Photos and video in encrypted conversations are not end-to-end encrypted yet, and a new device does not see messages from before it joined; both are coming.
