Notes you can trust. Write something down against a customer, a deal, a quote,
an invoice, an appointment, a job or an agreement — and when somebody changes
it later, what it said before is kept. A note is often the only record of what
was agreed on a phone call, and a record that can be quietly rewritten is not
worth much.

Choose who sees each one: the whole team, only you, or the customer as well.
The private ones are genuinely private — a colleague cannot read them, edit
them, or see they exist.

Mention a colleague and they hear about it once, through the same notification
settings they already chose. Fixing a typo afterwards does not tell everybody
again.

Pin the one thing everybody needs to know before they open their mouth, and it
stays at the top whatever the dates say. Notes about a customer show on their
timeline whatever they were attached to, so a note on their invoice is still
part of their story.
