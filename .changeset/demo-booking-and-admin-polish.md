Booking an appointment is easier to follow. On the booking page each available time is now a button you can pick, and a visitor who has not signed in chooses the time first and then verifies their email for that time, instead of being sent to sign in before they can choose anything. Times are listed once per day with a readable time zone such as "Pacific Daylight Time (Vancouver)" rather than "America/Vancouver", and the calendar name only appears when more than one calendar has openings. In the public demo, a visitor can now reserve a time with a made-up address such as you@example.com and lands on the appointment's own page; a real-looking address is turned away with a note to use a made-up one. Booking on your own site still requires a verified email. In the admin, "What changed" now reads as business news ("A shopper started a cart", "A flagged upload was held for review") and leaves internal housekeeping to "Include background work"; the demo's visitors can open the inbox and see enquiries sent through the contact form; a getting-started checklist no longer describes steps your role cannot see; and the page editor and the voice and video settings no longer reload their content in the browser with a hydration error.
