Hand a finished gallery over as one download. The client asks, a job packages
the files the gallery's download policy actually permits — marked renditions
for a watermarked gallery, web-sized where that is the policy — and the
archive is served through their session. Two photographs sharing a filename
stay two files, and a gallery too large for the format is refused rather than
packaged wrong.

Notifications carry the moments that matter: the gallery is ready, the client
sent their choices, the round was approved.
