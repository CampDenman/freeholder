Freeholder can now answer "what can I book?".

The answer is worked out fresh every time it is asked, from your opening hours,
the days that break them, what is already booked, and the busy time synced from
your own connected calendar. Nothing is cached, because a cached answer is a
double-booking waiting to happen.

Buffers are taken seriously. If a service needs fifteen minutes either side, or
forty-five minutes of travel, a slot that would leave you without that time is
never offered — not offered and then refused.

Services that need two things at once work properly. A treatment needing a
therapist *and* a room only shows times when both are free, and will use your
second room when the first is taken. It picks them together rather than picking
the person and hoping a room is spare.

If several people can do a job, a customer asking for one of them by name sees
that person first without the others being hidden. If you would rather spread
the work, round-robin shows each time once and gives it to whoever has least on.

Classes count places rather than slots: a group of three is not offered a
session with one seat left.

Your notice period, how far ahead people may book, and a daily cap if you set
one are all respected.
