Members can now add photos and video to posts, comments and messages, up to four at a time, each with a description for people who can't see it. A post can be just photos. Files go through the normal media upload, so they're checked and scanned. A file is only ever shown to people who can see the post, comment or conversation it belongs to. Deleting the post, unsending the message, leaving the conversation or blocking someone takes away access, even to a link someone already has.
