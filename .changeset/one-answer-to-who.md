An audience now means the same thing everywhere. The groups you define under
Segments are what campaigns send to, what automations let in, and what reports
can be cut by — one definition, so two screens can never disagree about who
"customers in Ontario who bought twice" are.

Automations gained the audience setting on their own page, and it now actually
holds: before this, an automation could be given an audience and would still
run for everyone, including the people it was written to leave out. It is also
pinned to the version you published, so narrowing an audience this morning does
not change what a run that started last night was doing.

Because an audience says a rule is about people, you can now build a manual
automation that acts on a contact — "do this for everyone in the group" — which
the editor previously refused.

Reports can be narrowed to an audience too: pick a group and the cohort report
shows what those customers went on to spend.

One fix worth naming: saving an automation's settings used to quietly clear its
audience, so renaming a rule could widen it to everybody without saying so.
