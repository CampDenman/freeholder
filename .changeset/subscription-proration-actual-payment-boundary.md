Require actual retained payment and applied-credit confirmation before a
subscription change's chosen instant. Late real money remains recorded for
fresh review; a supplied payment date or provider object creation time cannot
revive expired consent. Entirely local acceptances that miss their commit
deadline roll back their new documents and credit promises.
