Storefront commerce blocks for the page editor. The palette now offers a
Product grid (pick a collection — manual or rule-driven — and the
collection page's own shelf renders on the page: sort links, optional
filter facets, crawlable pagination, add-to-cart on every card), a
Featured collection (the collection's hero plus its curated front), a
Product search block (term search scoped to the page, facets and sort
included) and a Buy button (one product's variant picker or direct add,
exactly as the shelf renders it). The product card block joins the same
buy flow, and the product detail block's buy panel is confirmed on it.
Collection and product blocks pick their subject from the admin's
anchored picker — the same choice list drives the form select and the
on-canvas Replace button — and product grids emit the collection pages'
ItemList structured data. Chrome ships in English, French, Spanish and
Arabic, and a composed page is browsable, filterable and shoppable the
moment it publishes.
