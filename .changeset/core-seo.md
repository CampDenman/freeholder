Your site now tells search engines and AI assistants what it is. Every page
carries its full address, a social preview card and structured data describing
your business; a sitemap and a robots file are generated from what you have
actually published, and `/llms.txt` gives answer engines a plain summary of the
business and its pages. Renaming a page no longer breaks the links people
already shared — the old address redirects to the new one automatically.
