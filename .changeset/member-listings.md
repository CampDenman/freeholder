Sites can now let members sell. Once the site turns on the member market under Admin → Members, members list items, services, digital downloads and rentals with a price from My listings in the portal, and can take a listing down or put it back up. By default the site reviews each new listing first and can reject it with a note the seller sees. Approved listings appear in the new Market page, with search and a type filter, and in a Shop on the seller's profile; they never mix into the site's own shop. Buying from members opens once seller payouts are set up; until then the checkout refuses member listings and buyers can message the seller.
