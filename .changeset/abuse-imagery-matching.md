Sites can now check uploaded images against known child sexual abuse material through Microsoft PhotoDNA. When it is switched on, a matching upload is blocked and held as evidence that cannot be deleted, the uploader is suspended pending review, and the owner sees it under Admin → Members with where to report it (Cybertip.ca in Canada, NCMEC in the US) and a place to record the report's reference. It is off until the site owner has a PhotoDNA agreement.
