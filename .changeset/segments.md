One way of saying who. Describe a group of people once — "customers in Ontario
who bought twice", "anyone tagged trade who has not been in for a year" — and
everything that has to pick people uses that same description. Price lists can
now target a group instead of a single tag, which is what a wholesale rate
actually means.

Before you save anything, the screen tells you how many people it would be and
shows you a few of them. Afterwards, you can point at any customer and ask why
they are in the group, and get the answer rule by rule rather than a shrug.

A group can be live or frozen. A live one always means whoever matches right
now. Freeze one and it stops moving, so the answer to "who did this go to in
March" is still right in April, even for the people who have changed since.

If a group asks about something the system cannot answer — a module you have
switched off, say — it refuses rather than quietly matching more people than
you meant.
