Buyers and sellers can now review each other once a sale is complete, either delivered or with a dispute settled. Reviews are double-blind: neither of you sees the other's review until you have both written one, or two weeks pass, so nobody can answer a rating in kind. Ratings and reviews show on each member's profile, with separate averages as a seller and as a buyer, and the person reviewed can post one public reply.
