Plugins can now be installed, isolated, listed from signed registries, and removed with a keep-or-purge choice. First-party gift-registry, print-on-demand, community, voice/video and marketplace plugins ship as `definePlugin` modules. `create-freeholder` and `@freeholder/templates` are real installers and Bench presets. Every Tier-1 recipe has install, verify, backup, restore, migrate and update steps. The owner import studio walks discover → preview → commit → reconcile → publish, and WordPress/generic-site parsers refuse private origins.
