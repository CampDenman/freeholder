Preserve an original WordPress reader's proved contact identity when an email address changes or is reused. Opaque source receipts distinguish WordPress instances, follow shared contact merge/undo, and retain an erasure fence without copying names, addresses or comment bodies. Every newly seen email-bearing historical reader waits for a freshly verified owner to choose the person, including when no CRM contact exists. Historical metadata never creates a login-linked CRM identity by itself; agents and API keys cannot supply that review. Anonymous comments without an email stay unbound. Owners can review a window of readers together, selecting each original reader and person explicitly. A stale or invalid selection rolls back the whole window; no historical email groups people automatically. Historical reader exports require recorded ownership. An originally unknown reader remains unknown when its source is retired through a privacy request. Reader evidence under a legal hold stays private after erasure and survives import rollback and parent-page purge; rollback reports retained pages.
