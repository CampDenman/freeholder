Preserve an original WordPress reader's proved contact identity when an email address changes or is reused. Opaque source receipts distinguish WordPress instances, follow shared contact merge/undo, and retain an erasure fence without copying names, addresses or comment bodies. Ambiguous addresses, legacy sources without receipts and originally unknown readers whose addresses now belong to existing contacts wait for a freshly verified owner to choose the person; agents and API keys cannot supply that review. Historical reader exports require recorded ownership. An originally unknown reader remains unknown when its source is retired through a privacy request. Reader evidence under a legal hold stays private after erasure and survives import rollback and parent-page purge; rollback reports retained pages.
