Connect an own-store Shopify app and import its paid orders as draft invoices
for review on the shared contact spine. Tokens renew before expiry, shop
identity is verified on every page, and retries preserve import checkpoints
without duplicating invoices. Concurrent connection handshakes are fenced.
The marketplace screen explains setup and import limits in English, French
and Spanish. Live merchant acceptance and refund reconciliation remain open
under C3.13.
