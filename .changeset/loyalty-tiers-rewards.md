Give loyalty points something to buy, and status levels worth reaching.

Rewards are redeemed into a real coupon your shop already honours at checkout —
not a discount the loyalty screen invented and nothing else understands. Where
a reward is something no code can hand over on its own, like a framed print,
the redemption says it needs issuing by hand rather than pretending it is done
and leaving the customer to find out at the till.

Status levels are worked out from the same history that explains the balance,
so nobody is in a tier their own record does not justify, and spending points
never costs somebody their standing. Promotions and demotions appear on the
customer's timeline.

Redeeming checks everything before it takes anything: the balance, the stock,
the per-person limit, the tier, and how old the account is — points farmed and
cashed out the same hour is a pattern you can now set a floor against.
