The page is now the canvas. In the page editor, click the text on the
rendered preview and type: headings, paragraphs, FAQ questions and
answers, video captions and button labels all edit where they render,
keeping their formatting as you type, and every keystroke still flows
through the same debounced autosave as the form panel. Click an image
and a Replace button on the picture opens the asset picker right there,
swapping the image on the canvas the moment you choose — no separate
preview step for ordinary edits. The form panel stays beside the canvas
for the advanced things: links, alignment, alt text, SEO.
