A site can now be launched from a launch spec, a short file describing the business, its brand colours, one priced offer and what is live versus planned. It becomes a working one-offer site with an offer page, contact and call-request forms and a status page. A new private preview mode keeps the whole public site behind an expiring signed link and tells search engines not to index it, so an owner can review the site before anything is published.
