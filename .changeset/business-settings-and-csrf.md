Your business details now live in the platform: name, tagline, what kind of
business you are, country, language, currency, timezone, units and which day
your week starts on. Everything public reads from that one record, so changing
your business name changes it everywhere at once rather than in six places.

First-run setup knows what it still needs and locks itself once finished, so
nobody can walk through the wizard again on a live site. Modules can be turned
on and off, except the core, which cannot be switched off — doing so would lock
you out of your own site.

Requests that change anything now have to prove they came from your own pages,
so another website cannot make your browser act on your behalf while you are
signed in.
