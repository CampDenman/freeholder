Members can now take all their data with them, or delete their account, from the privacy centre, which is linked from a new "Your data" section in member settings. The export includes the member's profile, posts, comments, connections, conversations with their messages, listings, sales, purchases and reviews. Deleting an account removes the member's content. Messages they sent show as removed in other people's conversations. The site keeps its sales records, but under an anonymous name, and deletion waits until money owed to the member is paid and any dispute is settled. Member pages load nothing from third parties.
