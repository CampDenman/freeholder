Members and customers can now sign in with a passkey. They add one under Passkeys in member settings, where they're prompted until they have one, and next time choose "Sign in with a passkey" on the sign-in page instead of waiting for an email link. The email link still always works, so a lost device never locks anyone out. Recovery codes, shown once after the first passkey, let a member remove a passkey they no longer have.
