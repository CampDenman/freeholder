Public pages now follow the BigDataSEO / RIBA contract more closely. Every
page gets a branded Open Graph image (or your own override), search engines
are pinged via IndexNow when you publish or rename a page, robots.txt keeps
checkout and filter URLs out of the index, sitemaps split at 50,000 URLs and
weight section indexes above leaves, and `/llms.txt` lists locations and
catalog offerings alongside pages. Product, location, event and newsletter
feeds are generated from the same public URL registry as the sitemap.
