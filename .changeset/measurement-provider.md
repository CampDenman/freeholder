You can now choose an outside measurement provider, with Paradise Modern as the first choice (`MEASUREMENT_PROVIDER=paradise`). It only runs for visitors who press "Allow analytics and measurement", and the privacy choice names the provider and explains what allowing it means. When one of those visitors sends an enquiry, it is counted once as a lead for the ads that brought them. Freeholder's own analytics still store no IP address or ad click ID, and nothing changes unless you choose a provider.
