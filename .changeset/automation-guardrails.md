Automations now ask permission before they act. One check, before the step that would do something: does this person still consent, is there budget left, does this need your say-so, and is it a reasonable hour where they are.

Anything triggered by something a stranger typed — a form, an inbound message — can only propose, never act, however you configured it. That limit belongs to the whole run, so it cannot be shed by passing through a harmless step first.

Quiet hours hold a message until morning rather than dropping it, and the run picks up exactly where it paused.
