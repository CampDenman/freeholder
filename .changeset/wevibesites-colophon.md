Every page now ends with a quiet colophon crediting WeVibeSites and giving a phone number to call. Freeholder is a demonstration of what WeVibeSites builds, so the number is there for anyone who wants it rather than pushed as a call to action. It renders outside the CMS footer, so it appears even on a site with no footer Section and cannot be removed by deleting a block.

The `callCta` block's validation message no longer offers a live phone number as its example. It suggested `+16393834662`, which is a real line that rings — shown to an editor in the exact field they were trying to fill.
