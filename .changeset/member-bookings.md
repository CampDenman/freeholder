Members can now take bookings for their services. From My listings, they open a live service listing for booking and choose the length, the place (online, in person or at the customer's) and the days and hours they're available. A "Book" section then appears on their profile. Customers book through the site's normal booking page, with the site's cancellation and rescheduling policies and an invoice. A paid booking is owed to the member through Payouts. Members see upcoming bookings on My listings, and bookings pause while the member can't be paid.
