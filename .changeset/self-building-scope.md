Scope: an owner will be able to talk to a builder from anywhere in their own
site — "make this section wider", "add a deposit to my booking form" — and have
it happen, with a preview before anything publishes and one-click reversal
afterwards. The same builder is reachable from the owner's own AI assistant, so
they can administer and extend their site from whatever tools they already use.

Whoever deploys their own copy chooses which intelligence does the building, or
switches it off entirely. It is off by default.
