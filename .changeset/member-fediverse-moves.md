Members can now move between fediverse accounts. To move here from an account elsewhere, list it under "Your accounts on other servers", then start the move on the old server. To move away, first name this account on the new one, then choose "Move my followers"; your followers elsewhere are asked to follow the new account. When an account you follow moves, you follow its new home automatically, as long as the new account confirms it is the same person.
