Contacts are browsable now. Search by name or email, filter by stage, and page
through them — and because filters live in the web address, a filtered view is
something you can bookmark or send to your bookkeeper.

Open anyone to see their details and their history: every quote, booking,
invoice and email will appear on that timeline as those parts arrive. You can
add and edit contacts by hand, and if you try to use an email address that
already belongs to someone, Freeholder tells you rather than quietly creating
a second record for the same person.

Forms no longer throw away what you typed when something is wrong with it.
