Referrals now pay. A conversion by a referred customer writes a commission against the referrer, held back until the programme's refund window closes and then approved automatically. A refund inside the window reverses it; a refund after the money has gone out writes a negative row that nets off the next payout batch instead of becoming an argument.

Payouts are batched, one line per person however many codes they have, and export as CSV in minor units for a bank or an accountant. Tax paperwork is tracked and prompted for above a threshold — the platform records it and does not file it.

Referrers see their own earnings in the customer portal, through the same query the owner reads.

A programme can pay its referrers in loyalty points instead of cash by setting its commission to "none": the conversion is still recorded, and a loyalty earn rule matching it grants the points.
