Buying from members is now protected. Sellers mark an order as sent, with a carrier and tracking number for physical items, from Sales in the portal. Buyers see what they bought under Purchases, confirm when it arrives (which lets the site pay the seller straight away), and can open a dispute if it didn't arrive or wasn't as described. Each sale has a private conversation for the buyer, the seller and the site. While a dispute is open the seller is not paid; the site reviews it under Admin → Members and either pays the seller or cancels the payout so the buyer is refunded.
