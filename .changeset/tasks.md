One list of what has to happen. A task can hang off a customer, a deal, a
quote, an invoice, an appointment, a project or an agreement — or off nothing
at all, because ringing the accountant is a real job and does not belong filed
under somebody's name. Whatever it is attached to, it opens from the list, and
if there is a customer behind it, it shows on their timeline too.

Project checklists are now part of the same list rather than a separate one, so
there is a single answer to what you are meant to be doing today.

Say when it is due and, separately, when you want reminding — the day before a
deadline is more use than the moment it passes. A repeating task comes back
when you tick this one off rather than on a clock, so a fortnight away leaves
one waiting for you instead of fourteen. Tasks that are late or due today show
up in the morning briefing, yours and anything nobody has picked up.

The whole screen works without JavaScript.
