Your site can now be published in more than one language. Translated pages live
at an address like `/fr-CA/services`, they stay the same page — same link, same
place in your menu — and a visitor who lands on a page you have not translated
yet reads it in your own language instead of hitting a dead end. A language
switcher appears in your site's footer as soon as a second language is turned
on, and search engines are told which languages exist for which pages.
