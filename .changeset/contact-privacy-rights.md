Add an end-to-end consent and privacy-rights workflow to the Contact spine.
Consent is now immutable, purpose/channel-specific evidence with an effective
preference service and a translated customer preference centre. Owners can
record, verify, start, fulfil, deny and audit access, portable export,
correction and erasure requests from a translated privacy desk.

Exports and receipts are checksum-backed JSON files delivered only through an
authenticated, no-store route and pruned after 30 days. Step-up-protected
erasure anonymizes the Contact, timeline, forms, analytics, audit and merge
history, revokes customer credentials, preserves marketing suppression, and
retains only explicitly documented legal scopes. A reflected registry gate now
requires every module that references a Contact to implement export and
erasure alongside merge handling. The same services power admin, portal, HTTP
API, OpenAPI and MCP callers.
