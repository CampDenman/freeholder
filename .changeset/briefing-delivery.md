Any playbook can now report into your briefing. Write the prompt, pick the
schedule, tick "report into my briefing", and what the work found turns up as
its own section each morning — in the agent's own words, not a summary of a
summary. If a run failed, the section says so, because a playbook you asked to
report on is one whose silence you would read as "nothing to report".

Each playbook gets its own section, so you can hide one without hiding the
others, and hiding never stops the work.

Your briefing also leaves the admin now. When it is ready, it goes out through
the notifications you have already set up — inbox, email, text or push,
whichever you chose — so a briefing from Monday still reaches you if you do not
sign in until Thursday. Quiet hours and digests apply as they do everywhere
else.

It only sends when there is something in it, and only once a day however many
times the day is rebuilt.
