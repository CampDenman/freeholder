Prepare immutable original-payment return allocations before money moves, record only the exact human-attested offline portion, and keep online refunds on their original provider account across retries. Close the return and issue its credit only after every portion is confirmed. Customers can request and follow their own returns in the four supported locales.

Owners choose whether received items go back into stock and can provide a safe return-label link. Stock restoration never exceeds units that actually shipped.
