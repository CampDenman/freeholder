You can connect a Gmail or Microsoft mailbox now, and Freeholder will learn who
has been in touch.

It reads headers only — who wrote, when, and the subject line. It never asks
your provider for a message body, so there is no body for it to store, show or
lose. It is not becoming a mail client: there is no inbox, no folders, no
compose screen.

What you get is the thing that was missing: the enquiry from three months ago
sits on the same timeline as the invoice. A correspondent becomes a contact
through the same door everything else uses, so connecting a mailbox does not
create a second address book — and if someone writes from a new address, they
go to your duplicate queue rather than being merged on a guess.

A name from an email header only ever fills a blank. It cannot rename someone
you have already named, whatever the sender put in their display name.

Subject lines are stored only for a mailbox you have explicitly shared with the
business. Connect your own mail and Freeholder learns who you correspond with,
not what about.

Connecting to read is a separate, read-only permission from connecting to send.
Neither one grants the other, and a mailbox is read by the person who connected
it — managing connections is not the same as reading someone's mail.
