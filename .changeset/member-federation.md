Members can now join the fediverse. Once the site allows it under Admin → Members, a member can choose Join the fediverse in their settings and get an address like @handle@yoursite that people on Mastodon and other ActivityPub servers can follow. Their public posts reach those followers, and deleting a post removes it there too. Followers-only, private and adult posts never leave the site. Leaving tells followers elsewhere the account is gone.
