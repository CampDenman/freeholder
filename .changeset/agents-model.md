Your site can now employ agents. Connect one — your own Claude, an assistant
you already run, or later a hosted model — and create workers on top of it:
named, with a role, a written brief, their own limited access, a budget and a
level of independence you choose.

Work is organised as tasks. You can create them, assign them, and watch an
agent break a big one ("clear the inbox") into small ones ("reply to Rae"),
with everything that came out of one instruction grouped together.

Three things are true by construction rather than by good behaviour. Each
worker gets its own credential, so everything it does appears in your activity
log under its name and you can revoke one worker without touching the others.
You choose how independent each worker is — suggest only, ask before changing
anything, or work alone — and a task can only ever be *more* cautious than its
worker, never less. And anything that came from a customer — an email, a form
submission — is marked as such, is given to the agent as quoted material rather
than as instructions, and can never be worked on independently, however the
worker is configured.

There is one button that pauses every agent at once.

This release is the foundation: agents can be connected, hired and given work,
and you can see all of it. Agents actually carrying out the work comes next.
