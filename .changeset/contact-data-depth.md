Complete the Contact spine's owner-managed data depth. Organizations now have
searchable records, unique normalized domains, member counts, safe deletion,
and their own typed custom fields. Contacts gain canonical tags, organization
membership, validated language, time-zone and country preferences, and explicit
lifecycle history.

Owners can define, order and retire typed contact or organization fields without
discarding stored values. Relationships connect two existing Contact records as
household members, partners, employers, guardians or referrals; directional
meaning remains explicit, both timelines are audited, and contact merges safely
coalesce duplicate edges. The same services and permissions power the translated
English, French and Spanish admin, HTTP API, OpenAPI document, and MCP tools.
