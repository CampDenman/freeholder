Members can now report anything that's wrong: a post, a comment, a message sent to them, a listing, a review or another member. Reports go to a single queue under Admin → Members, where site staff can remove the content, suspend the member responsible, do both, or dismiss the report. Several reports about the same thing are grouped and decided together. Removing or suspending requires a reason, and the member is told what was removed. People who reported it hear that action was taken, and the reported member is never told who reported them. Every decision is recorded on the audit trail.
