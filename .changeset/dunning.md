When a membership renewal cannot be billed, the plan now has a dunning
policy: retry on the days you name, keep access for a grace window, send
a notice on the channels the person already receives, then pause, cancel
or move them to a cheaper plan. Paying the open invoice stops the chase
and restores access. Without a policy the failure is still written on
the history and nothing else happens.
