Buyers can now pay for what members sell, and sellers get paid. A buyer can mix items from several sellers in one cart and pays once. For each sale, the seller's share (less the site fee, which is zero unless the site sets one) goes on a payout ledger. By default the site holds it for a window it chooses, so buyers are protected, then it becomes due; site owners can instead pay sellers instantly. Sellers enter their payout details on the new Payouts page in the portal, which keeps them encrypted, and they can sell once the site confirms them. Sellers see every amount held, due and paid. Site admins set the payout model, holding window, fee and provider under Admin → Members, release payouts early, record payments with a reference, and resolve anything a refund affects. A refund cancels what is still owed; a refund after payment is flagged for the site to sort out. This release pays sellers by hand; Stripe Connect and other providers come next.
