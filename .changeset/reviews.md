Collect and publish customer reviews. Ask once after a purchase or booking,
moderate what comes back, reply in public, and show a review wall with photos.

The rating is the careful part: withholding a review keeps it out of the wall
but still counts it, so moderating can never quietly improve the number. Only
reviews judged not to be a customer's opinion at all are excluded, and the wall
says when there are more reviews than it is showing.
