Light and dark are both standard now, everywhere. Choose one or leave it on
"match system" and your device decides — and if you leave it on system, it
keeps following your device when you change that later, rather than freezing
whatever it was the first time you visited.

Your choice is remembered and applied before the page is drawn, so a dark site
never flashes white while it loads, and the scrollbars and dropdowns your
browser draws match the theme too. The switch works with JavaScript turned off.
