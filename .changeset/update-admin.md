Updates now have a screen. It opens with the one line that matters — "Up to
date", "Update available", or "2 security releases behind — CVSS 8.1" in the
danger colour — and gives you the release notes and one button. Below that:
what the signed feed offered and whether each release can be applied from your
version, the policy editor for channel, night window, drain and snapshot
retention, a pause switch, what updating and rolling back actually mean on
your deploy target, your fork's drift if you have one, and every update this
instance has ever attempted.
