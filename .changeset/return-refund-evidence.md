Returns can no longer be marked refunded by inventing a card-provider refund or recording only part of the amount owed. Recording an offline refund now requires a freshly verified person to confirm that the full amount was returned; it covers the original installments exactly once. Return quantities also account for earlier returns and reject duplicate lines. Card-provider refunds continue through their original payment provider, and complete return-to-provider reconciliation remains open (C5.19/C11.10).
