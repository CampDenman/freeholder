Pages no longer hide the rest of an article in the browser. A paywall is a
rule that selects content, and a grant answers for the person looking. Hard
walls show a teaser. Soft walls show a short lead-in. Metered walls give
the same number of free views to readers and to search engines. Signed-in
contacts can open a registration wall. The gated copy is simply not in the
HTML until it is allowed.
