The member market now has a published prohibited-items policy at /market/policy, linked from the market. Until the site owner writes their own under Admin → Members, a starter policy applies, covering weapons, drugs, counterfeits, stolen goods, unsafe products, live animals, hazardous materials, personal data, exploitation, and adult content unless the site allows it. Sellers accept the policy before their first listing, and again whenever it changes.
