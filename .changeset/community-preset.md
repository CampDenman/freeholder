There's a new "community" preset for sites whose product is their members. It gives a home page with Join, the member feed and the market; community guidelines that point to the market policy; About and Contact pages; a Supporter pass product; and a welcome email. Choose it with `create-freeholder --preset community` or when installing a preset. Members' settings stay your choice under Admin → Members.
