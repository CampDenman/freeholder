The customer app now knows its own map: every screen a customer can reach,
which of your services it is allowed to ask for, and what it says when there is
nothing to show. Links land where they should — a push about a ready gallery,
a link your customer tapped in an email, or a pasted address all open the same
place, and a link for a different business is refused rather than quietly
switching which business they are looking at.
