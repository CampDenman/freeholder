Public forms can now be checked by PM Shield (`PARADISEMODERN_SHIELD=on`) as
well as Freeholder's own spam traps. Accepted enquiries can also be copied into
your Paradise Modern CRM as leads (`PARADISEMODERN_LEADS=on`). A flagged
enquiry goes to the spam queue, where you can still rescue it. If Paradise
Modern is unreachable, the enquiry is kept and the lead copy is retried later.
