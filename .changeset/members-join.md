People can now become members of a Freeholder site. A signed-in visitor picks a handle and a display name at `/join`, confirms they meet the site's minimum age and says whether they are an adult; no birthdate is ever asked for. Members edit their own profile under Member profile in the portal. Site admins choose under Admin → Members whether anyone can join, joining needs approval, or joining is by invitation, set the minimum age, invite people by email, and approve, suspend (with a reason the record keeps) or reactivate members. Every member action is also available to agents through the API and MCP. This is the first step of the multi-member platform in MASTER.md §44.
