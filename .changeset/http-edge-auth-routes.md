The platform now answers over the web. You can create the owner account on a
fresh install, sign in, stay signed in as you browse, and sign out — and
signing out ends your own session and leaves your other devices alone.

Your sign-in is kept in a cookie your browser will not hand to any script and
will not send to another website, and it renews itself while you are working
rather than logging you out mid-task. A wrong password, a request you are not
allowed to make, and a genuine fault are each answered distinctly, so the site
can tell you to log in when that is the problem and stop pretending everything
is fine when it is not.
