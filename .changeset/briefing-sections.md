Your daily briefing now has things in it.

It tells you what is on today, pulled from the calendars you have shared with
the business — and where a calendar does not share detail, it says "busy"
rather than quietly showing a title you asked it not to store.

It tells you about new enquiries since yesterday morning, so a Monday briefing
covers the weekend, and about invoices that are past due, showing what is still
owed rather than what was originally billed.

Most importantly, it tells you when the platform itself is unhappy: an AI agent
waiting for you to approve something, work that failed, a webhook that switched
itself off after too many failures, a connected account that needs
reconnecting. All of those are quiet by design — nothing retries forever and
nothing wakes you at 3am — which is exactly why they are collected here, once a
day, where you will actually see them.

Sections you have nothing in are left out entirely. A quiet day says so.
