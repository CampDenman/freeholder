Paid event tickets are now taxed like every other sale, under the site's tax
settings, using the business's location and the event's venue. An event with
no venue of its own is taxed at the business's location. If the venue is
typed in as free text rather than chosen from the business's saved locations,
no tax is added and the invoice says why; choose a saved location as the venue
to have tax worked out there. The tax appears as its own line on the invoice
the attendee pays, as it does for store orders.
