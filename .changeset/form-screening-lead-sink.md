You can now add an outside spam-screening service to your forms, with Paradise Modern as the first choice (`FORM_SCREENING_PROVIDER=paradise`). It can only hold a suspicious enquiry for your review and never throws one away, and if the service is down your forms work exactly as before. Separately, and off unless you turn it on, accepted enquiries can be copied to an outside CRM (`LEAD_SINK_PROVIDER=paradise`); they are always kept in Freeholder too. While either is on, every form tells visitors which service receives what and why.
