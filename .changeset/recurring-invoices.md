"freeholder": minor

Invoices that come round again. Set up a retainer or anything else you bill on
a cycle — weekly, monthly, quarterly or yearly — and Freeholder raises each
period's invoice for you. Each one is its own invoice with its own due date and
its own receipt, which is what makes your aged-debtors list mean something.

It raises a draft by default. Sending an invoice to a customer with nobody
looking is the one thing you cannot take back, so it waits for you unless you
tick the box. And if Freeholder is off for a fortnight it picks up from now
rather than sending a fortnight of invoices when it wakes up.

Editing a schedule changes what the next invoice says, never what an issued one
said. Putting your retainer up in March does not re-issue February.

Overdue invoices now actually go overdue. The status existed but nothing set
it, so an invoice sailed past its date and still said "sent" until you noticed;
it is swept hourly now.

And you can chase. Pick a nudge before the due date and one or two after, and
Freeholder sends them — except to anybody who has already paid, which it checks
at the moment of sending rather than when you set it up.
