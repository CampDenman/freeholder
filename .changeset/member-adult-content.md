Site owners can now allow adult (18+) content under Admin → Members. It is off by default. When it's on, adult members can mark a post or a listing as adult, and it shows an 18+ badge. Only adults who turn on "Show me adult posts and listings" in their member settings ever see it. Minors and visitors never do: not in feeds, profiles, the market or product pages, and they are never mentioned or tagged in it. Turning the setting off again hides everything already marked as adult.
