One set of message templates for everything you send: receipts and confirmations, campaigns, newsletters, texts, and anything an automation writes. Same editor, same variables, same translations.

A template says which values it needs — the invoice total, the customer's first name — and sending refuses if one is missing, rather than letting a customer read `{{invoice.total}}` in their receipt.

Templates that ship with Freeholder keep their original wording alongside your edits, so "put it back how it was" works even after an update, and you can see at a glance which ones you have changed.
