Your site can now take enquiries. Build a form in the admin, drop it onto any
page, and what people send you arrives as a contact with the message on their
timeline — the same person whether they write to you once or five times.
Obvious spam is caught by two traps that ask your visitors to do nothing at
all, and is held in a review queue rather than deleted, so a real enquiry
wrongly flagged is one click from being rescued.
