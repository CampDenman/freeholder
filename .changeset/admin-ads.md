Ads have their own admin screen. Define the positions you sell and which sizes each reserves per screen size, record who is buying, create campaigns with a price and a schedule, and say what runs where.

A campaign carries an approval state you can see and set, because a sponsored placement should never reach a page without somebody having said yes.

The screen states plainly that nothing is being served yet — inventory and sales exist, and creatives, house fill and counting are still to come — so a live campaign showing no impressions reads as the next piece of work rather than a bug.
