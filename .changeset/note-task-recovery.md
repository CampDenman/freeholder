C7.02/C7.03/C11.14: Move notes and tasks to recoverable trash, preserving their IDs, revisions and contact links. Trash stays out of normal lists, search, reminders and briefings; authorized staff can restore it or confirm permanent deletion. Retention holds protect both manual and scheduled purge, and privacy erasure cannot be undone through recovery. Contact merge and undo now also update contact-subject links. A localized, paginated trash screen and view/manage-aware controls support the workflow; a daily sweep removes eligible trash after thirty days. Project checklists and their counts exclude trashed tasks, and task navigation and saved views use the task grant.
