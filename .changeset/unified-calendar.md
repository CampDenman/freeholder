There is a calendar in the admin now. It shows one week at a time, with every
connected calendar merged into a single view of when you are already committed.

There is nothing to click into, because there is nothing behind a block. The
page draws times and only times — no titles, no guests, no notes — so it is
safe to leave open in a meeting or on a shared screen.

Underneath it, the times a calendar has taken are now available to the rest of
Freeholder as one merged answer. Booking will use exactly this, which means a
private appointment can block a slot without anything about it being readable
by the part of the system that offers the slot.

The page also lists every calendar that is *not* blocking time and says why —
because it is personal rather than shared with the business, because you marked
it ignored, or because it needs reconnecting. That is the list worth reading
when something got double-booked.

Weeks are drawn in your business's timezone, including the two days a year that
are 23 and 25 hours long.
