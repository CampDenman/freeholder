Sell advertising on your own site, if that is your business. Define the
positions where ads appear, place them on pages like any other block, and the
space is reserved at every screen size — so an ad arriving late never shoves
the article down the page.

An advertiser is a contact like everybody else, so the local bakery buying a
banner and the local bakery buying prints is one person in your records, not
two.

Campaigns carry their own dates, rates, budgets and priority, and a paid one
cannot go live until it has been approved. Your own house promotions skip that,
because they are yours already.

Line items say what runs where: which positions, which pages, which countries
and devices, which days and hours, and how often one person should see it.
