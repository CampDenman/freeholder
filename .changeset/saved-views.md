Keep the list you keep going back to. Filter a list the way you want it, give
that a name, and it sits along the top ready to open again — "Ontario customers",
"unpaid over 30 days", "mine, due this week".

A saved view is just this page's address with a name on it. That means it works
the way you would expect a web page to: the back button behaves, you can
bookmark it, and you can paste the link to a colleague and they see what you
see. Nothing is hidden in the app that the address does not say.

On the contacts list you can also choose which columns you want, and that
travels in the link too.

Share a view and anyone here can use it; only you can change yours. Pick one to
open first and it opens first — for you. Somebody else picking a different one
does not take yours away.
