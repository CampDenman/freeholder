Referrals have their own admin screen. Set up the programme and how commission is calculated, issue codes to the people sending you business, build a payout run and download it as CSV for your bank, and record who still owes you tax paperwork.

What is payable now and what is still held back are shown as two separate numbers, because the holdback is exactly the difference between what somebody has earned and what you owe them today.

Before approving a payout run you can see who is on it and for how much — previously only the total was readable, which made approving a payment run a guess rather than a decision.
