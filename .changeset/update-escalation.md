A security release you have not applied now comes and finds you. How long it
waits depends on how bad it is — a CVSS 9 interrupts after a day, a CVSS 2
after a week — and it keeps telling you even while automatic updates are
paused, because pausing was only ever safe on the condition that the platform
still says you are exposed. Your own assistant can ask too: every update
service is an MCP tool, and reading update status is a different API-key scope
from applying one, so a monitoring key cannot cut your site over.
