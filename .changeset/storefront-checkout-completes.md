Shoppers can now finish checkout, see prices before the cart, and know when something was added.

- Downloads, vouchers, services and passes no longer ask for a postal address or a delivery method. New variants of those products are marked as not shipping; physical, rental and bundle products still ship, and you can change any variant yourself.
- When checkout refuses an order, the fields that need attention are marked, the message says what to fix, and everything the shopper typed is still there. If no delivery method reaches an address, the shopper is told plainly and pointed to pickup or to you.
- Pickup methods are now offered at checkout. Picking up at your counter is offered whatever the shopper's address, since it does not depend on where they live.
- Product cards and product pages show the price ("From CA$45.00" for products with options). Options are listed with their own prices, one is chosen already, and the Add to cart button is always in view.
- Adding to the cart now shows "Added to your cart" with a link to the cart, announced to screen readers. Cart lines show the variant's name instead of an internal code.
- The Orders list in the admin shows each customer's name and email instead of an ID.
- The demo store now has a Canada shipping rate and pickup at the studio, so its prints and calendar can be bought.
