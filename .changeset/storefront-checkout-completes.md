Shoppers can now finish checkout, see prices before the cart, and know when something was added.

- Downloads, vouchers, services and passes no longer ask for a postal address or a delivery method. This applies to variants you already have: updating marks every variant of a digital, service or pass product as not shipping. New variants follow the product's kind, and changing a draft product's kind updates its variants. Physical, rental and bundle products still ship, and you can change any variant yourself.
- When checkout refuses an order, the fields that need attention are marked, the message says what to fix, and everything the shopper typed is still there. If no delivery method reaches an address, the shopper is told plainly and pointed to pickup or to you.
- Pickup methods are now offered at checkout, to addresses in the shipping zone you put them in. To offer pickup to anyone, put it in a zone with no countries. Local delivery is no longer offered at the storefront checkout, which does not say which location it ships from. Delivery methods are listed cheapest first. When more than one applies, the shopper must choose one; checkout never picks for them. If they submit without choosing, the methods are shown and checkout asks them to pick, with or without JavaScript.
- Product cards and product pages show the price ("From CA$45.00" for products with options). Options are listed with their own prices, one is chosen already, and the Add to cart button is always in view.
- Adding to the cart now shows "Added to your cart" with a link to the cart, announced to screen readers. Cart lines show the variant's name instead of an internal code.
- The Orders list in the admin shows each customer's name instead of an ID. Staff who can read contacts also see the customer's email.
- The demo store now has a Canada shipping rate and pickup at the studio, so its prints and calendar can be bought.
