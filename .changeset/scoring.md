Lead scoring you can actually read. Say what earns points — viewed the pricing
page, opened an email, accepted a quote — and how long those points last. Every
rule is on one screen, in full, and nothing scores anybody any other way.

Open a customer and you see their number and every reason for it, line by line,
each one showing what it was worth when it happened and what is left of it now.
The lines add up to the number. There is no hidden model and no stored score to
go stale.

Points can fade. A page view from last week says more than one from last year,
so a rule can state how long its points last and they fall away steadily rather
than vanishing overnight.

Rules can move people along: accept a quote and become a customer, or reach a
score you choose and become a prospect worth calling. Nothing ever moves
somebody backwards — opening an email will not demote a customer to a lead.

You can also give points by hand, with a reason, and it shows up in the same
list as everything else.
