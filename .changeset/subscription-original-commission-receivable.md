Preserve a subscription change's original merchant commission when applied
credit leaves too little new cash to collect the remaining fee. Review the
separate bounded receivable and record actual remittances or original receipt
returns through the shared money services. Customer principal and merchant
commission collection remain distinct; this source requires its complete
guarded schema and qualification before use.
