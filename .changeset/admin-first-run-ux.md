The admin is easier to find your way around on day one. Screens are grouped
into a few named sections (Start, Website, Sell, Bookings & work, Customers,
Marketing & results, Settings & system) in a sidebar on a computer and behind
a single Menu button on a phone, so a page's content starts near the top of
the screen. Each admin screen now has its own browser-tab title.

The overview's first suggested task is now something you can see on your
site (publishing a page, adding a customer) rather than an alerts routine,
and it moves on once you have done or skipped it. "What changed" shows what
people did; the platform's own background housekeeping is behind "Include
background work".

Plainer words throughout: "Header & footer" instead of "Chrome", "Page link"
instead of "Public address", coupon discounts entered as a percentage, module
names instead of internal keys, and teammates' names instead of account ids
in comments and history. Currency, time zone and language fields are lists to
choose from instead of codes to type. Form labels use the normal typeface and
fields line up. In Media, file type, fingerprint and scanner status are under
"File details". The text block explains that HTML belongs in a Custom HTML
block.

Accessibility: on the public demo the skip link is the first thing the
keyboard reaches, the banner offers the public site when you are already in
the admin, and the page preview no longer shows the banner. The privacy
choices button no longer covers form fields on a phone, 404 pages and the
customer sign-in page have proper titles, and the notification channel list
is valid for screen readers. The editor's preview fills the window instead of
a short fixed frame.
