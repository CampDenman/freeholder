Write an agreement once and send it as often as you like. Templates take
placeholders — put `{{customer_name}}`, `{{business_name}}` or `{{today}}`
anywhere in the text and Freeholder fills them in for whoever you are sending
it to. Anything it cannot fill stays visible in the draft, so you spot it
before your customer does rather than sending "Dear ,".

Saving a template always makes a new version. Whatever you have already sent
keeps saying exactly what it said, which is the whole point of a signed
document, and retiring a template stops you sending it without erasing the ones
you already have.

Agreements you both sign are now possible. Mark a template as mutual and, once
the customer has signed, you countersign it yourself — in that order, because a
document only you have signed is an offer rather than an agreement.

Every agreement can be exported: the words, who signed, when, from where, and
the fingerprints that let anyone check the text has not been altered since. It
is plain text on purpose — it will still open in thirty years.
