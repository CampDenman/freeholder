"Sorry, we're full" now has a queue behind it. Somebody who wants a slot that
is taken can join the waitlist for a window rather than an exact time, and when
a seat frees it is offered to the first person in line and **held for them**
until their deadline passes — not announced to everybody at once. Lapsed offers
release themselves and pass the slot on. The queue is yours to reorder at
`/admin/calendars/waitlist`.

Classes and group bookings gained the rest of what they needed: add a guest by
name alone, mark who turned up, and remove somebody to give the place straight
back to the class. Moving an appointment into a class that is already full is
now refused, the same way booking into one is.

Cancellation terms are now attached to the appointment when it is booked, so
editing your policy changes what the next customer agrees to and never what the
last one already did. Cancelling or recording a no-show applies those terms and
tells you plainly what it came to — the fee, what is owed back, what is still
owed — without moving a penny by itself. Settling stays a deliberate act on the
invoice.

And customers can now do it themselves. The link in their confirmation opens
their own appointment: their time in their own timezone and in yours, the terms
that apply, and buttons to move or cancel it. No account, no password, and no
email to you.
