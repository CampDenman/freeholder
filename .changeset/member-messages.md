Members can now message each other, one to one or in groups, from the new Messages page or the Message button on any profile. A message from someone you do not follow and are not friends with waits in Requests until you accept it, and the sender cannot tell whether you read it. Read receipts are opt-in, and you only see other people's while you share your own. Blocking someone closes your conversation with them, you can remove a message you sent for everyone, and you can mute or leave any conversation. Messages are encrypted when stored; they are not yet end-to-end encrypted, and the Messages page says so. End-to-end encryption is planned as the next step for messaging.
