Run a loyalty programme where every point can be explained. Points are a
ledger rather than a number, so "I had 400 stars last week" is a question with
an answer: the balance is the sum of its rows, and a customer can see every one
of them — what earned it, when, and which order it came from.

Earning happens by watching the customer's own history, so nothing in your shop
or your booking calendar has to know the programme exists. A refund takes the
points back by writing the reversal next to the original rather than by quietly
editing the past.

Points never expire without notice — the programme refuses to be configured
that way — and the owner can see, at any moment, what the outstanding points
would cost if everybody spent them tomorrow.
