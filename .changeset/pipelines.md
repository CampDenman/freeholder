A pipeline, if you want one. Freeholder does not assume you do — a shop never
opens a deal — so nothing appears until you press the button, and then you get
the standard stages, fully editable, with as many boards as you need.

The deal board shows what you are working on, what each stage is worth, and a
forecast weighted by how likely each one is. Set the odds once on a stage and
every deal in it inherits them; override a particular deal only when it is
unusual. Losing a deal asks why, because the moment you lose it is the only
moment you know, and that answer is the only thing a lost deal is still worth.

The lifecycle board shows who is where — subscriber, lead, prospect, customer,
repeat, advocate — and you can rename or reorder any of it. Moving somebody
keeps the lifecycle your price lists and reports read exactly in step, so the
two can never quietly disagree.

Everything works without JavaScript, so the board is usable on a phone with a
bad signal.
