Appointments exist. Book one at /admin/appointments — pick a calendar, a time
and a customer, and it lands in the diary.

The customer is matched by email against the contact you already have. Someone
who booked with you last year is the same person, not a second record.

Two things about a time being taken. First, Freeholder refuses to double-book,
and it refuses in the database rather than in the screen — so two people
clicking at the same moment for the last slot cannot both succeed. Second,
back-to-back is allowed: an appointment may start exactly when the last one
ends.

A class or a group session works differently on purpose. Give a calendar a
capacity and it takes that many people at once, counts the seats, and says how
many are left when someone tries to book past it. A guest with no email address
can be added by name — "and my sister" is a real booking.

Appointments move through confirm, start, done, no-show and cancel, and a
finished one cannot be quietly reopened; you book a new one instead.
Cancelling asks why, because that reason reaches the customer. Moving an
appointment keeps a record of where it was.

Everything that happens shows up on the customer's timeline, next to their
invoices and enquiries.
