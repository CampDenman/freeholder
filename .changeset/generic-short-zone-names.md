Event times in North American zones now end with the zone's everyday abbreviation: "PT", "MT", "CT", "ET", "AT" or "AKT". They used to show a summer or winter abbreviation such as "PST" or "PDT". Those depend on daylight-saving rules, and rules change. British Columbia stays on UTC−7 all year from 1 November 2026, and Alberta and Manitoba also stopped changing their clocks. The formatting library still labels winter times in Vancouver "PDT", in Edmonton "MDT" and in Winnipeg "CDT". Every other zone name is unchanged, for example "CET"/"CEST", "GMT+1" or "UTC". The times themselves are unchanged, and calendar files still carry exact instants.
