Members now have public profiles at `/@handle` and can connect with each other. Each member chooses who sees their bio and links (everyone, followers and friends, friends only, paying subscribers, or only them), whether their account is private, and whether search engines may show their profile; new accounts start private and unindexed. Members can follow (a private account approves each follower), send and accept friend requests, and block, mute or restrict anyone. A block hides each side from the other and ends follows and friendship both ways. The portal's new Connections page lists followers, follows, friends and requests, with approve, decline and undo. All of it is available to agents through the API and MCP.
