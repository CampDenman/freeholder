Freeholder now has a face. A fresh install opens a three-step setup that
creates your owner account, asks who the business is, and gets out of the way —
and choosing your country fills in your currency, time zone, units and which
day your week starts on, all still editable.

The look is one consistent system rather than a pile of styles: every colour,
size and spacing is a named setting, so rebranding a site will be a save rather
than a rebuild, and the phone app will match the website by construction. Text
is set in Schibsted Grotesk, served from your own site — no fonts are fetched
from anyone else, so nothing about your visitors leaks to a third party.

Every colour pairing in the interface is checked against accessibility contrast
minimums by an automated test, in both light and dark.
