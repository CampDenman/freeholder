Forgotten passwords can be reset by email now: ask for a link from the sign-in
screen, choose a new password, and every device is signed out. If your site has
no email set up yet it says so plainly rather than leaving you waiting for a
message that is not coming. Freeholder can also send through your own mail
server, which is what makes the new-enquiry notifications on your forms
actually arrive.
