You can now send account mail and newsletters through Paradise Modern. Set
`MAIL_ADAPTER=paradise` or `MAIL_BULK_ADAPTER=paradise` with your site key.
Every message says why it is being sent. A newsletter goes only to people
whose sign-up Freeholder has on record, and it keeps your own unsubscribe
link. When someone unsubscribes, Paradise Modern is told too.
