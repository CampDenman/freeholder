## Why the physical fee needs an atomic endpoint

Two genuine captures can be1/fee0 and4199/fee400 against accepted4200/fee400.
If receipt UUID execution order puts4199 first, its legacy per-prefix floor is
399. That does not invalidate the bank's actual400/0 fees. The new policy keeps
each exact original receipt/PI/charge/fee/recipient identity and validates the
whole genuine batch before any write. It never infers capture chronology from
UUIDs, timestamps or amounts.

The accepted bank/target protocol remains `controlled_net_gross.v1`. Only the
NEW signed application-header protocol `controlled_cash_atomic.v1` selects the
atomic policy; old signed headers retain their exact body/signing purpose and
legacy prefix semantics. There is no public policy argument or allow flag.
Unknown application versions refuse, including in ordinary HMAC readers.

For original actual cash C, allocated fee F, accepted cash principal P and
physical fee target D, the original committed prefix must still satisfy
F=floor(D*C/P). Each receipt's actual bank fee is proved independently before
the complete requested pending set is checked. Its sum must equal
floor(D*(C+batchCash)/P)-F. Every actual pending bank-positive receipt must be
explicitly selected; overage, missing receipts, fee refunds, reversed recipient
funds, foreign ownership, unfinished callers and other raw debt remain refused.
BigInt/numeric arithmetic retains cent residuals and safe-integer bounds.

The immutable per-receipt prior cash/fee fields are actual earlier sums. A later
receipt may use them only after those exact earlier real payments have settled
in the SAME transaction. A prefix with a temporarily different floor cannot
commit: the original complete deferred guards require every actual receipt and
the exact final cash/fee endpoint, or roll the entire canonical/core3 TX back.
No future or unseen receipt can fund a residual.
