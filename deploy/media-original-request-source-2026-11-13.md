transaction. Earlier intents cannot obtain a fresh writer birth/token. Physical

orchestrator handoff keeps caller tokens out of service schemas, audit inputs,
public outputs and resumable job payloads. Earlier completion retries refuse
current-key HEAD adoption until exact original recovery is implemented.
