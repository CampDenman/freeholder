# Original buyer agreement erasure source — C12.38/C1.08

This is an isolated source increment on the qualified exemption fence 089d2b34.
It does not activate new provider schedules, apply the staged commercial-terms
schema, change a published migration, or close acceptance.

The existing erasure cohort schema already distinguishes merchant-owned tax
profiles from buyer-owned subscriptions. The original worker accepted only the
merchant-profile variant. The new shared authority reads the verified original
request and exact current job/cohort membership. Buyer authority follows the
original subscription contact and frozen tax profile, even when that buyer does
not own the seller's business declaration. Merchant authority remains separate.
Missing original parents, scope rows or durable original enqueue inventories
stay recovery debt.

Two private services prepare actual per-agreement cancellation jobs and complete
one agreement after original physical-caller, stopped-resource, invoice and
money proof. Completion validates the canonical artifact checksum, source and
job membership and current holds; destination minimization and actual task ACKs
share one transaction. Corrupt artifacts commit the existing recovery transition
and return recovery to the outer worker. Late physical/cash evidence includes
buyer cohorts in the existing durable repair admission.

The shared cancellation worker consumes these actual cohorts, restricts buyer
inventory to their original agreement and checks the original enqueue snapshot
again before admitting a physical cancellation. Provider calls remain outside
transactions. Positive observations and caller completion remain independent.

The existing privacy source guard intentionally remains closed in this increment.
Before wiring it to prepare, commerce.money must defer customer deletion and
exemption/credential minimization until these schedule tasks actually converge.
That composition needs a real money-finisher task, exact original artifact job
membership, existing hold coupling, and actual provider-customer jobs appended
before the money task is acknowledged. The implementation must also consume the
current common returned-unapplied disposition; it cannot invent a paid invoice
for returned cash. Nonempty invoice inventory is still pending in the existing
worker, and no source-only helper proves those provider outcomes.

Finite qualification prepared here: seven DB-free original-authority controls,
existing original-inventory/caller-proof tests and private/audit inventories.
Types, lint and these controls have not yet run on this increment. Native follow-up
must prove: a buyer different from the merchant; one actual cancellation with
no duplicate while its caller is paused; current hold refusal; missing/restored
original inventory; corrupted artifact preserving positive facts; and a late
positive after an actually completed buyer erasure. Fresh assembled terms schema
is required before destination minimization can be exercised.
